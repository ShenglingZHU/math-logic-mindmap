## Mathematical content

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x\in\mathbb{Q}\\
&\quad\Rightarrow\quad\exists m,n\in\mathbb{Z}\\
&\qquad\text{such that}\quad\left\{\begin{aligned}
&n>0\\
&\gcd(m,n)=1\\
&x=m/n
\end{aligned}\right.
\end{aligned}
$$
