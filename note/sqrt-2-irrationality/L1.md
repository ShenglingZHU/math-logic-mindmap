## Incoming relations

### R1 → L1

[[note/sqrt-2-irrationality/R1|R1 · Reduced representation exists]]: A reduced fraction represents $\sqrt{2}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\exists m,n\in\mathbb{Z}\\
&\quad\text{such that}\quad\left\{\begin{aligned}
&n>0\\
&\gcd(m,n)=1\\
&\sqrt{2}=m/n
\end{aligned}\right.
\end{aligned}
$$

Input contribution: A reduced fraction represents $\sqrt{2}$.
