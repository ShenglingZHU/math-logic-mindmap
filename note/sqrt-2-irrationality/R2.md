The witness statement records $a,b\in\mathbb{Z}$, $b>0$, $\gcd(a,b)=1$, and $\sqrt{2}=a/b$. Positivity gives $b\ne0$, so the quotient and the squaring step are legitimate.

## Conditions and verification

- Condition: $a,b\in\mathbb{Z}$; check: Both variables are bound by the witness introduction.
- Condition: $b>0$; check: The reduced fraction theorem supplies a positive denominator.
- Condition: $\gcd(a,b)=1$; check: The reduced fraction theorem supplies coprimality.
