#### Input formula C3 K2

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\forall z\in\mathbb{Z},\\
&\quad 2\mid z^2\\
&\qquad\Rightarrow 2\mid z
\end{aligned}

Passed after combination to [[note/sqrt-2-irrationality/R4|R4]]: The even numerator is used in the denominator argument and final contradiction.

$2\mid a$

The square relation makes $a^2$ even. Since $a\in\mathbb{Z}$, the parity theorem gives $2\mid a$.

Role of this input: The square equation gives an even numerator square.
