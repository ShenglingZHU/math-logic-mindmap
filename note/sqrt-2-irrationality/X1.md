# X1 · Coprimality contradiction

Type: `CONTRADICTION`

## Navigation

Stage: [[note/sqrt-2-irrationality/index#Stage S5 · Contradiction and discharge|Contradiction and discharge]]. Both witnesses are even despite being coprime; close the witness and rationality scopes in order.

Role and purpose: The greatest common divisor is both one and at least two.

Motivation: The witness is coprime, yet $2\mid a$ and $2\mid b$ make two a common divisor, forcing $\gcd(a,b)\ge2$. This directly contradicts $\gcd(a,b)=1$.

Position: Intermediate step; scope: Reduced fraction witnesses.
