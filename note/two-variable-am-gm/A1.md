#### Input formula C2 K2

$$
\begin{aligned}&\forall u,v\in[0,\infty),\\&\quad u\le v\\&\qquad\Rightarrow\sqrt u\le\sqrt v\end{aligned}
$$

| Symbol | Mathematical definition or role | Range / unit | Meaning / interpretation (if applicable) |
